## The frame

One frame is the film's reason for existing. Everything below is arranged to
deliver it.

> **`INSUFFICIENT_BASIS`, in yellow mono, at the exact coordinates where every
> previous verdict has appeared — beneath a fully intact apparatus, with five
> decision rules greyed and never reached.**

It occurs at the end of Movement VII. It is a substitution, not a reveal: its
power comes entirely from something else having occupied that position six times
before. The apparatus must read as complete and correct, because the point is
that the refusal is what the system correctly produced, not what it failed to
avoid.

### What must be installed for it to land

| Requirement | Installed in | Consequence if missed |
|---|---|---|
| A fixed verdict locus, identical coordinates every time | II, held by every plate after | The substitution is invisible; the frame is just a word |
| Yellow meaning *the current assertion*, without exception | II onward | A refusal in yellow reads as an error state, not an output |
| A decision cascade the viewer has watched run in full | VI | "Five rules never evaluated" carries no weight |
| An apparatus recognisable as unchanged from VI | IV, V, VI | The refusal reads as breakage rather than design |

### Constraints that protect it

> **Rule F1.** No yellow may mark an absence anywhere before Movement VII. The
> visual language permits it (§1.4); that permission exists for this frame and
> must not be spent early.

> **Rule F2.** Nothing in the film may state, caption or narrate that the system
> can decline. If language announces it, the frame illustrates a sentence the
> viewer has already accepted and violates nothing.

> **Rule F3.** No plate before VII may carry a comparable void, and no plate
> after VII may hold longer. VII is the longest hold in the film.

> **Rule F4.** Movement VI must resolve satisfyingly. Its structural job is to
> reinforce, one last time, the expectation that VII breaks.

## The rule this document is built on

> **The End State of every movement is the Start State of the next, verbatim.**

Where two movements meet, the text is identical. This is not a formatting
convention. It is the structural claim of the film: there is no cut between
movements, no re-establishment, no scene that begins somewhere new. The film is
one continuous state, advanced nine times.

A movement earns its place only if the frame it ends on raises exactly one
question, and only the next movement answers it. Where that fails, the movement
is wrong — not the order.

## Movement I — Volume

**Question**
What does an institution decide from?

**Purpose**
Establish the actual input, at its actual scale, before any claim is made about
processing it. The viewer must feel the quantity as a fact of the work, not as a
problem being sold to them. Nothing is characterised as broken here. Nothing is
promised.

**Start State**
Empty plate. Graphite ground, drafting grid at 6%. No structure, no data, no
label. Slug only.

**End State**
The plate is filled edge to edge with source material rendered as structure —
several hundred hairline rectangles, tiled, each one a document. No content is
legible. Quantity is legible. The count sits in mono at the lower right. Nothing
moves.

**One emotional word**
Weight

## Movement II — Opacity

**Question**
What happens when one process reads all of it?

**Purpose**
Show the incumbent method honestly and without caricature, then let its single
deficiency surface on its own. The deficiency is not that the answer is wrong —
the film never claims that. It is that the answer arrives with nothing behind
it, and the viewer has no way to check it. This movement must create the demand
that the rest of the film satisfies. It does not editorialise.

**Start State**
The plate is filled edge to edge with source material rendered as structure —
several hundred hairline rectangles, tiled, each one a document. No content is
legible. Quantity is legible. The count sits in mono at the lower right. Nothing
moves.

**End State**
All source material has collapsed into a single solid rectangle at plate centre.
One line leaves it. At the end of that line, at the coordinates this film will
use for every verdict from here on, is a conclusion set in mono. The conclusion
is marked in yellow. Behind it there is nothing — no evidence, no citation, no
structure. The rectangle has no interior. The plate holds.

**One emotional word**
Doubt

## Movement III — Structure

**Question**
What if the material had a common form?

**Purpose**
Introduce the canonical intermediate representation as the film's first
construction. The teaching point is that translation is not reasoning: the
material changes form and gains no conclusions. Entities, claims, evidence and
relationships appear as distinct, labelled kinds — the viewer learns the
vocabulary here and will need it for every movement that follows.

**Start State**
All source material has collapsed into a single solid rectangle at plate centre.
One line leaves it. At the end of that line, at the coordinates this film will
use for every verdict from here on, is a conclusion set in mono. The conclusion
is marked in yellow. Behind it there is nothing — no evidence, no citation, no
structure. The rectangle has no interior. The plate holds.

**End State**
The solid rectangle has opened. In its place is a structured graph: entities,
claims, evidence items and the relationships between them, each kind visually
distinct, each carrying a mono identifier. Evidence items connect to the claims
they support. No conclusion is present anywhere on the plate. The structure is
complete and still.

**One emotional word**
Order

## Movement IV — Isolation

**Question**
What if no reader could see the others?

**Purpose**
The pivot of the film. Decompose the reading into independent kernels and make
their separation visible as an absence — there is no channel between the lanes,
and the viewer must be able to confirm that by looking. The consequence follows
immediately and is the reason the architecture exists: because no lane can
observe another, two lanes arriving at the same position is information rather
than echo. This movement earns everything after it.

**Start State**
The solid rectangle has opened. In its place is a structured graph: entities,
claims, evidence items and the relationships between them, each kind visually
distinct, each carrying a mono identifier. Evidence items connect to the claims
they support. No conclusion is present anywhere on the plate. The structure is
complete and still.

**End State**
Four sealed lanes run the height of the plate. The structured graph has entered
each lane in full. Each lane has produced a structured artifact carrying
findings and the evidence identifiers behind them. **No line connects any lane
to any other.** Two lanes have independently arrived at the same position on a
shared axis; that coincidence is marked in yellow. The lanes remain sealed.

**One emotional word**
Separation

## Movement V — Derivation

**Question**
Where does confidence come from?

**Purpose**
Show that confidence is computed and never claimed. The derivation runs visibly,
rule by rule, and terminates in a band with its limiting factor named. Two
properties must be legible without narration: the band takes the weakest
evidence item rather than the average, and the institutional band takes the
minimum across lanes rather than the mean. The viewer should be able to see a
strong result being held down by a weak input and understand why that is
correct.

**Start State**
Four sealed lanes run the height of the plate. The structured graph has entered
each lane in full. Each lane has produced a structured artifact carrying
findings and the evidence identifiers behind them. **No line connects any lane
to any other.** Two lanes have independently arrived at the same position on a
shared axis; that coincidence is marked in yellow. The lanes remain sealed.

**End State**
Each artifact now carries a derived confidence band, expressed as a value step
on bone rather than a hue. Beside one artifact the derivation is printed in
full — numbered rules, in mono, each showing its input and its effect,
terminating in a band and a named limiting factor. Below the four bands, the
institutional band has resolved to the lowest of them, not their average. The
limiting factor is marked in yellow.

**One emotional word**
Honesty

## Movement VI — Contradiction

**Question**
What happens when they do not agree?

**Purpose**
Show disagreement surviving as disagreement. Where two lanes hold opposed
positions on the same topic, the opposition is rendered and retained — never
averaged into a middle value, never resolved by majority. The distinction this
movement establishes is required for the next one: a contested judgment rests on
strong evidence pointing both ways, which is not the same state as a judgment
resting on too little evidence.

This movement has a second, structural job, per Rule F4. It is the last
reinforcement of the expectation that Movement VII breaks. The machine must be
seen resolving a genuinely hard case, completely and satisfyingly, with the full
decision cascade evaluating in order — several rules reached, one firing. The
viewer must leave this movement certain that this system produces verdicts.
Without that certainty, VII violates nothing.

**Start State**
Each artifact now carries a derived confidence band, expressed as a value step
on bone rather than a hue. Beside one artifact the derivation is printed in
full — numbered rules, in mono, each showing its input and its effect,
terminating in a band and a named limiting factor. Below the four bands, the
institutional band has resolved to the lowest of them, not their average. The
limiting factor is marked in yellow.

**End State**
Findings are grouped by topic. On one topic the four lanes have split: positions
sit on opposite sides of a stated axis, both well supported, neither withdrawn.
The tally is printed in mono with its running totals visible. The decision rules
are listed in order, each showing whether it fired and what it observed; the
rule that fired is marked in yellow, and the rules that did not fire remain on
the plate at reduced opacity. The verdict reads `CONTESTED`.

**One emotional word**
Friction

## Movement VII — Restraint

**Question**
What happens when there is not enough?

**Purpose**
The summit of the film. The same structure runs against a thin packet, and the
system stops rather than producing an answer. This must read as a designed
capability, not a failure state — the pipeline is intact, the reasoning is
sound, and the output is a refusal. Coverage gaps are drawn rather than omitted,
so that what was not evaluated is visibly not the same as what was evaluated and
found false. This is the beat the film exists for.

**Start State**
Findings are grouped by topic. On one topic the four lanes have split: positions
sit on opposite sides of a stated axis, both well supported, neither withdrawn.
The tally is printed in mono with its running totals visible. The decision rules
are listed in order, each showing whether it fired and what it observed; the
rule that fired is marked in yellow, and the rules that did not fire remain on
the plate at reduced opacity. The verdict reads `CONTESTED`.

**End State**
The same four lanes, in the same positions, the apparatus visibly unchanged from
the previous movement. The packet is thinner. Most claims stand in dashed
outline — reached, not established. Two lanes have concluded nothing and say so.
The first decision rule fires immediately; the remaining five are never
evaluated and stand greyed and unreached. At the verdict locus — the coordinates
every conclusion in this film has occupied — the mono string
`INSUFFICIENT_BASIS`, marked in yellow. The unevaluated material stays on the
plate, dashed, at reduced opacity. Nothing further is produced. The plate holds
longer than any other in the film.

**One emotional word**
Relief

## Movement VIII — Generality

**Question**
Does any of this depend on the subject?

**Purpose**
Establish that the object being introduced is a primitive rather than an
application. The same core runs material from unrelated domains, with different
packet shapes and different vocabularies, and the core does not change. The
measurement is shown as a measurement — the hold-out domain is identified as
such, because a claim of generality that was not tested against a control is not
evidence.

**Start State**
The same four lanes, in the same positions, the apparatus visibly unchanged from
the previous movement. The packet is thinner. Most claims stand in dashed
outline — reached, not established. Two lanes have concluded nothing and say so.
The first decision rule fires immediately; the remaining five are never
evaluated and stand greyed and unreached. At the verdict locus — the coordinates
every conclusion in this film has occupied — the mono string
`INSUFFICIENT_BASIS`, marked in yellow. The unevaluated material stays on the
plate, dashed, at reduced opacity. Nothing further is produced. The plate holds
longer than any other in the film.

**End State**
The camera has pulled to `INDEX`. Four packets of visibly different shape sit
side by side, from four unrelated fields. Each has run the same core; the core
is drawn once, in the centre, unduplicated. The lines from every packet enter
the same structure. A count of changes made to that core is printed in mono, and
it reads zero. One of the four packets is labelled as the hold-out.

**One emotional word**
Recognition

## Movement IX — Record

**Question**
What remains?

**Purpose**
Close on the property the viewer has been watching hold for eight movements
without being told: nothing was deleted. Every defeated claim, withdrawn
position, unfired rule and unevaluated item is still on the plate. The film ends
by naming what it has already demonstrated, and then stops. No capability claim,
no forward promise, no logo resolve beyond the mark itself.

**This movement resolves the frame at VII; it must not compete with it.** Both
plates trade on yellow, and the distinction has to be exact. VII is yellow
*present*, marking a refusal, over an apparatus stripped to dashed outlines. IX
is yellow *absent* entirely, over a record that is visually full. Emptiness with
an assertion, answered by fullness with none. If IX reads as the more striking
plate, it is over-designed and should be quieted, not VII amplified.

**Start State**
The camera has pulled to `INDEX`. Four packets of visibly different shape sit
side by side, from four unrelated fields. Each has run the same core; the core
is drawn once, in the centre, unduplicated. The lines from every packet enter
the same structure. A count of changes made to that core is printed in mono, and
it reads zero. One of the four packets is labelled as the hold-out.

**End State**
A single completed record on one plate: the structure, the evidence, the
artifacts, the derivation, the fired and unfired rules, the defeated positions
at reduced opacity, the unevaluated material in dashed outline, and the verdict.
Nothing has been removed. The record digest is printed once, in mono. The plate
is still. There is no yellow anywhere on the frame.

**One emotional word**
Permanence

## Why the order is locked

The structure is not a sequence of topics. Each movement is the only available
answer to the question its predecessor's final frame raises.

| Test | Result |
|---|---|
| Remove IV | V has no artifacts whose confidence could be derived. The chain breaks. |
| Remove V | VI has no confidence for the lanes to disagree about. The chain breaks. |
| Remove VI | VII becomes unreadable — a viewer cannot distinguish *too little evidence* from *conflicting evidence*, and the film's central distinction is lost. |
| Remove VIII | The film is a demonstration of one application. The primitive claim is unsupported. |
| Swap VI and VII | Declining for want of evidence is shown before the viewer has seen what sufficient evidence looks like. The refusal reads as weakness rather than design. |
| Swap III and IV | Kernels are shown reading a form that has not yet been introduced. |
| Move VIII before VII | Generality is claimed before the system has been seen to hold under pressure. It reads as scope, not as evidence. |

The final frame carries no yellow. For eight movements yellow has meant *this is
what is being claimed right now*. Its absence at the end is the last thing the
film teaches: the claiming is finished, and the record is what is left.
