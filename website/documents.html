    Every numbered document YUKTI has produced, published as written. Nothing here has been rewritten for a public audience: each page is derived from the research document or architecture decision it names, and cites that source by path.

    Most of this work is not accepted. Status is stated on every entry, and reserved document numbers with no drafted content are listed at the foot of this page rather than quietly omitted.

    Published
    An approved ruling that governs.
    Active
    A complete research document. Drafted, not accepted.
    Open
    A proposal or open question. Not a finding.
    Superseded
    A ruling that no longer governs.

    RESERVED

    Numbers allocated, not yet drafted

    These identifiers are taken. Each is a 0-byte placeholder in the repository, held against research that has not been written into it. They are listed so the numbering reads as complete, and so no future document silently claims one of them.
